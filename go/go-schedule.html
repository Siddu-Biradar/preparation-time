📅 Go Backend — 24-Week Daily Schedule

Realistic plan for an SDE2 frontend at IBM transitioning to Go backend. 5 hours/day, 7 days/week. Aligned with go-roadmap.html.

Duration: 24 weeks (6 months) Total: ~840 hours 5h/day × 7 days Target: ₹30L+ Go roles

🗓️ Your Daily Template (every day, including weekends)

Same structure every day. Consistency > intensity. Don't break the chain.

11:00 AM – 1:00 PM

🌞 Morning Slot — Learn (2h)

  • 0:00–1:15 — Watch the day's tutorial(s) from roadmap
  • 1:15–2:00 — Code along + take 5-line notes
Fresh brain = concept absorption. No DSA here.
9:30 PM – 12:30 AM

🌙 Night Slot — Apply (3h)

  • 9:30–10:30 — DSA: 1 NeetCode problem (Go) ⭐
  • 10:30–12:00 — Build: project work / extend morning concept
  • 12:00–12:30 — Review, commit to GitHub, plan tomorrow
30m buffer to 1 AM if deep in flow.
⏰ Weekly recap (Sunday 9:30 PM): Skip Sunday's DSA. Use that hour to: (1) write one paragraph "what I learned this week" in a learning log, (2) push that week's project work, (3) plan next week's tickets.

⚠️ Reality checks before you start:
  • Day-job conflicts: Some weekdays you'll have IBM crunch. Do bare minimum (1 DSA + 30m morning) and don't break the chain. Catch up Saturday.
  • Skip Sunday DSA in Sundays — use that hour for review. 7-day grind without ANY break = burnout by week 8.
  • Don't skip projects. Tutorials feel productive but interviews ask about your code, not your video count.
  • If you fall 2+ weeks behind by Week 12, this is normal — cut Phase 12 (AI+Go) and Service Mesh entirely. Ship 2 projects instead of 3.
🎯 The 80/20 if you only have 12 weeks (not 24): Skip Vim mastery, generics deep dive, Fiber, NATS, RabbitMQ, Kustomize, Operators, Service Mesh, Phase 12 entirely. Keep: Go fundamentals, concurrency, net/http + Chi, Postgres + Redis, JWT, testing, Docker, K8s basics, Prometheus, system design. Ship 2 projects (URL shortener + chat). That alone = job-ready.
